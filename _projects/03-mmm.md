## Overview

Built a **Marketing Mix Model (MMM)** for an Argentine wine company to quantify the contribution of marketing activities to commercial performance and support investment decisions.

The project focused on understanding how different marketing activities contributed to outcomes over time and translating those insights into actionable recommendations for marketing investment.

---

## Business Problem

Marketing investments were distributed across multiple activities and channels, making it difficult to quantify their individual contribution to business performance.

The objective was to build a data-driven framework that could:

- Quantify the contribution of different marketing activities.
- Separate marketing effects from other factors affecting performance.
- Understand how marketing investments translated into commercial outcomes.
- Support decisions on how to allocate marketing resources.

---

## Marketing Mix Model

The model combined historical commercial and marketing data to estimate the relationship between marketing activities and business outcomes over time.

The analysis considered:

- **Marketing investments** across different activities.
- **Commercial performance** over time.
- **External drivers** affecting demand and performance.
- **Time dynamics** and delayed effects of marketing activities.
- Differences in the contribution of individual marketing activities.

The resulting model provided a framework for evaluating the expected impact of alternative investment scenarios.

---

## Scenario Analysis

The model was used to evaluate alternative marketing investment scenarios and understand their expected commercial impact.

The workflow allowed the team to:

1. Estimate the contribution of individual marketing activities.
2. Evaluate historical marketing effectiveness.
3. Simulate alternative investment allocations.
4. Compare expected outcomes across scenarios.
5. Support marketing investment decisions with quantitative evidence.

---

## Decision Support

The final analysis translated model outputs into actionable recommendations for marketing investment.

Rather than focusing only on model performance, the project emphasized making the results interpretable and useful for decision-makers:

- Contribution of marketing activities.
- Relative effectiveness of different investments.
- Expected impact of alternative scenarios.
- Opportunities to reallocate marketing resources.
