I’m **Carla Peñafiel Messmer**—engineer and economist turned **Lead Data Scientist**. I almost failed my first-year programming course, and today I write code for a living. My motto: **don’t let anything define you**.

I originally planned to pursue academia (I shaped my degree path toward a PhD), but I fell in love with **advanced analytics** and **machine learning**. I still get that spark of surprise when a model works beautifully—and lately I’m having a lot of fun with **agentic** workflows. I make choices with rigor and intuition; I trust my gut when it points to what I truly want.

A fun fact: I’m a lifelong **The Sims** fan. In a job interview I shared how I like pushing systems to their limits—curiosity even led me to meet the **Grim Reaper** in-game just to test boundaries. Years later my manager told me, “we need that kind of curiosity on the team.” I smiled; I never thought that story would land me the job.

I’ve volunteered for years at **NiñasPro**, helping girls get started with programming—today I’m a partner there. I’m good at organizing teams and making things happen. Current interests: ML for industrial operations, time-series forecasting, operator-facing visualization, and practical data products people actually use.
